01 Ship Chandler London

Marine Supply at the Port of London

Ship Chandler London provides end-to-end maritime procurement and professional vessel provisioning for ships calling at UK terminals. We collaborate closely with fleet managers, shipowners, technical superintendents, purchasing executives, ship masters and local shipping agencies to guarantee seamless, punctual onboard delivery throughout the vessel's port turnaround.

Our mandate is to act as a unified, dependable agency desk for every vessel supply requirement in UK waters. Whether delivering premium galley provisions, bonded goods, deck consumables, or technical engine-room spares, our dispatch desk synchronizes each order precisely with the ship's confirmed ETA, berth assignment, port timetable and individual requisition list.

We operate strictly as dedicated marine suppliers rather than general freight forwarding agents. Our core specialization centers exclusively on ship chandlery: fresh food provisions, galley stores, deck and engine supplies, bonded stores, crew beverages and cabin consumables.
